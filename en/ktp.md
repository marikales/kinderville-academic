# Lessons per module · 2026/27 (for your teaching plan)

*How many lessons of each subject fit into each module. Module I shows the exact count against the current timetable (±1 — the cost of a possible day change); for Modules II–V the timetable may be rebuilt, so we give an honest min–max range: **the lower bound is the worst case after a rebuild** — the lesson lands on the leanest weekday, and, if the subject can end up in a late slot, all half-study days are lost from it too. Any subject can end up in a late slot except the core subjects of classes with Light children (Y3-2, Y4, Y5 — those always sit in lessons 1–4); in the classes without Light children (Y2, Y3-1, Y6) — literally any. Electives are always in the afternoon, so their upper bound loses half-study days as well.*

**What the numbers mean.** The numbers are all the lessons of the subject that stand in the timetable on the module's school days (public holidays, breaks, teacher-development days and closed event days — 9 Oct, 18 Dec, 9 Apr, 25 Jun — are already excluded). **Tests are inside these numbers**: a test takes up one of the listed lessons, no extra hour is added. So plan your topics for: lessons minus tests minus reserve.

**Reserve — always.** Don't plan topics for every lesson on the list: keep 1–2 lessons per module in reserve — for illness, an outing, or a topic that went deeper. Better to plan less and reach the end than to race through the programme.

**Tests sit on top of the topics.** Checks and tests are not counted in the topic list: they take about 1 more lesson per module (up to 2 for Russian and Mathematics). Give them their own line in your plan.

## Worth knowing

- **Monday is the leanest day of the year: 34 school Mondays against 37–38 for every other weekday.** Anything scheduled on a Monday loses 3–4 lessons a year. Right now the Russian science lesson of four classes out of six sits on Mondays (Y2, Y3-1, Y5, Y6; Y3-2 has Wednesday, Y4 — Tuesday).
- **Science drifts apart between the languages.** Russian science sits in morning slots everywhere and is safe from half-study days; English science for Y3-2, Y4 and Y5 sits in late slots and loses them. Year totals (Russian / English): Y2 — 34/37 and Y6 — 34/37 (more English: the extra lessons run as review), Y3-1 — 34/34, Y3-2 — 38/32, Y4 — 37/32, Y5 — 34/30. Y5 has the widest gap; Y3-2 misses six English lessons against Russian.
- **Y3-1 consistently gets the least of everything** — its lessons cluster on Mondays and in late slots.

**About half-study days** (10–11 & 17 Dec, 12 Feb, 1–2 & 8 Apr, 24 Jun): lessons 1–4 run as normal, **all late lessons (5th and 6th) are cancelled** — this affects electives, English science for Y3-2/Y4/Y5, Cambridge Math and English (extension). These losses are already built into the lower bounds and into the exact Module I numbers.
