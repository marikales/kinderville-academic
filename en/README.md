# Kinderville Teacher Portal

Working materials for the school: programmes, yearly planning, assessment criteria, lesson frameworks.

**How to use this.** Everything here is a ready-made proposal — take it and teach. If something doesn't sit right with you, suggest a better way: we'll talk it through and quite possibly change it.

> ### 📌 [1 September · Welcome Day](schedule/sep1.html?lang=en ':ignore')
> The first day runs on a special timetable, not the usual one: assembly with parents, curator time, four meet-the-teacher sessions, with rooms and lunches by stage. Please look up your own day in advance.
