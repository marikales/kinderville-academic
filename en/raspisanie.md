## Bells from 2 October 2026

The break before the last lesson is 20 minutes (14:55–15:15): the upper stage gets back from swimming, has a snack and starts lesson 6 on time. The first day on the new bells (1 October) showed that a 15-minute teachers' lunch is far too short, so from 2 October everything after lunch moves 5 minutes later: teachers' lunch 12:50–13:10 (20 minutes), reflection 16:10–16:25.

| | Lower (Y2–Y3) | Upper (Y4–Y6) |
|---|---|---|
| Lessons 1–3 | 9:05 · 10:05 · 11:05 | 9:05 · 10:05 · 11:05 |
| Lunch | 11:55–12:25 | 12:50–13:10 |
| Prep | 12:25–13:00 | 13:10–14:00 |
| Lesson 4 | 13:10–14:00 | 12:00–12:50 |
| Lesson 5 | 14:05–14:55 | 14:05–14:55 |
| Long break and snack | 14:55–15:15 | 14:55–15:15 |
| Lesson 6 | 15:15–16:05 | 15:15–16:05 |
| Reflection | 16:10–16:25 | 16:10–16:25 |
| Teachers' lunch | 12:50–13:10 | 12:50–13:10 |

**Swimmers (Mon/Wed/Fri).** Upper stage: prep ends at 13:40 — the children change and leave for the pool at 13:45; the driver is at the pool at 14:45, back at school by 15:00 — snack and lesson 6 at 15:15. Lower stage: leave at 15:05, during the long break; back at school by 16:10, in time for reflection.
